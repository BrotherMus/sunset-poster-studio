Sunset Poster Studio turns your own photos and a caption into multi-panel crime-epic poster art: tilted panel grids with thick borders and thin gold or neon frame lines, sunset and neon colour grades, film grain and glow, and a huge striped sunset title with a neon script sub-line. Panels can also show painted scenes (a sports car at sunset, a marina speedboat, a neon motel sign, a palm highway, a neon skyline, a beach sunset). Poster extras make it read like a release poster: a role tag on each panel, location / time stamps, a press quote with five stars and a VOL badge, a round seal, a tagline bar and a credits block with a barcode, each one editable or switchable. Crop each panel, hit "Surprise me" for a new look, and export a PNG at 1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1350 or 1920 × 1080, all offline in the browser.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```
